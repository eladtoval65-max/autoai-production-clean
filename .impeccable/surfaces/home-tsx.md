# Home (/) — AutoAI finder

Mode: Persuade (landing + the working finder tool on one page; tool states inherit the world).
Audience: Israelis choosing a new or used car, mostly on phones, before talking to a seller.
Action: answer the questionnaire in the first viewport; multi-select steps end with Continue.
Proof: the TCO mechanism working on a catalog car with provenance labels; no invented claims, testimonials or counts.
Constraints: Hebrew RTL primary + English toggle; insurance = estimate; consent before leads; chat rate-limited.
History: first build ("Israeli street at noon", seed:89f8b2751b88e93b) was rejected by the owner as too local. Owner then delegated the look to research; he took the category standard (standing exit).

## Direction contract

THESIS: The car you can actually afford every month, shown the way the best international car-comparison sites show cars. Refuses dark showroom heroes and locally-themed costumes; convention is the commitment, executed at Carwow/Autotrader craft.

OWN-WORLD: White page, cool mist (#f3f6f9) section grounds, deep navy ink, one savings-green action colour (#0a7c59) used only for actions and money. Rounded 12–24px cards with soft two-layer shadows, pill buttons and tabs. Rubik for headings and figures, Assistant for body. Provenance code: solid navy = official, green hatch = estimate, dashed outline = missing.

STORY: The visitor sees monthly cost as the headline number, trusts it because every figure says where it came from, and finishes the questionnaire.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Start column: headline, sub-copy, three true trust facts, then the questionnaire card (progress bar, 2-column answer tiles, Continue). End column: large rounded car photo with a floating white card overlapping its bottom, holding the leading car, its green monthly figure and the coded breakdown.

FORM: the category standard (standing exit), benchmarked on Carwow, Autotrader UK and Edmunds. seed: none (unavailable)

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
